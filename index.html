Scientific interpretation & limitations

Absorbance A is dimensionless; the nth derivative with wavelength in nm has units nm⁻ⁿ. Local polynomial derivatives, especially the 3rd/4th, are sensitive to noise, window size, nonuniform sampling and edge effects. The original data are retained. No peak, crossing, isosbestic point or zero-crossing is assigned automatically. Curve overlap is a visual comparison, not chemical identity evidence. PNG physical width is configurable in inches or cm with embedded DPI; SVG is resolution-independent. A missing reading is not silently bridged.
